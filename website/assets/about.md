I love watching and playing sports, especially basketball. I wanted to understand
why some NBA players keep building on a breakout season while others give back
part of the improvement the following year.

The project began as a computer science class assignment and continued as
independent research. It grew to include comparisons across performance
dimensions and player characteristics, sensitivity checks, and this interactive
website. My goal is to let other basketball fans explore the evidence and give
researchers a clear view of the assumptions behind it.
